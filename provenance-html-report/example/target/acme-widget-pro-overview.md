# Acme Widget Pro overview

## What it is

Acme Widget Pro is a handheld widget for small workshops and schools. It runs for up to 12 hours on a single charge and weighs 450 g.

## Features

- Waterproof, so you can use it anywhere.
- Pairs with the Acme mobile app over Bluetooth and receives firmware updates over the air.
- Comes with a two-year warranty.

## Price and availability

Acme Widget Pro costs $199 and goes on sale on 1 March 2027. Schools get a 10 percent discount.

It's the lightest widget on the market.
